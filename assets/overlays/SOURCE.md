# Perfect Overlays (source)

From https://github.com/ourigen/Perfect_Overlays (640x480 overlays for
handhelds). Derived from the work of 1playerinsertcoin, with bezels by
mugwomp_93 and drkhrse (https://github.com/drkhrse/drkhrse_miyoo_bezels).
The upstream repository carries no license file; it is included here with
the project owner's approval and credited as above.

The 720x480 variants are generated from the 640x480 originals: CRT and
full-screen art is scaled to 720x480, while bezel side art is widened and the
screen window keeps its original pixel size. They are a starting point for
per-panel remakes. The GBA has no default 720 bezel because 720x480 is exactly
3x its 240x160 screen.
